Put your article cover images in THIS folder.

Then, in relevant-news.jsx, set the article's "image" to just the
file name, e.g.:

    image: "fy2027-budget.jpg"

…and it will load from  news-images/fy2027-budget.jpg  automatically.

Recommended: landscape images, ~800×450px (16:9), JPG or PNG, < 300 KB.
